**Adding a photo:** export at 800×600 (cards) or 1600×900 (hero), JPEG quality ~80, aim for
under 300 KB. Update `src`, `width`/`height`, and a descriptive `alt` in `index.html`.
The files here are resized, cropped, and compressed for the web. Full-resolution originals are not in this repo; ask a maintainer if you need one.
